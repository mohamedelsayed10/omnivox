# Northwind Cloud: product notes

Northwind Cloud offers a free trial that lasts fourteen days. No credit card is required to start it.

## Pricing

The Starter plan costs nine dollars per user per month. The Team plan costs nineteen dollars per user per month and adds shared workspaces and single sign-on. Annual billing gives two months free.

## Support

Support is available by chat from eight in the morning to six in the evening Cairo time, Sunday to Thursday. Enterprise customers have a named account manager and a four hour response target.

## Data residency

Customer data is stored in the region selected at sign-up. Moving a workspace to another region takes about one business day.
